## Research Vision
### From Crop Ecophysiology to Predictive and Deployable Technologies
- We investigate how crops perceive and respond to environmental conditions throughout their life cycle, from germination and vegetative growth to heading, grain filling, and harvest.
- We translate physiological and ecological mechanisms into quantitative indicators, predictive models, and crop production technologies.

## Integrated Research Approach
- Field experimentation, physiological analysis, and molecular and metabolic profiling
- Digital phenotyping using ground-based sensors, imaging platforms, drones, and satellite data
- Integration of crop models, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence
- Development of solutions for open fields, agrivoltaic systems, controlled environments, and space agriculture

<br>

## Research Themes

### Climate-smart crop production
- Environmental regulation of crop phenology, growth, and yield formation
- Physiological mechanisms and mitigation strategies under heat, low light, drought, flooding, ozone, and related climate extremes
- Biostimulant-based technologies and optimization of treatment concentration and timing
- Agrivoltaic crop production through optimized phenology, management, and productivity under altered light and microclimate conditions

### Digital phenotyping, crop modeling, and AI
- Image- and sensor-based quantitative indicators of crop growth and development
- Integration of field, drone, satellite, and crop-model data
- Predictive models and decision-support tools for phenology, productivity, and climate risk

### Controlled-environment and space crop production
- Resource-use efficiency and edible biomass productivity in closed controlled environments
- Multi-harvest production strategies using ratoon rice technologies

### Field implementation and standardization
- Field validation of crop management and stress-mitigation technologies across diverse environments
- Standardized protocols and integrated technology packages for practical application
