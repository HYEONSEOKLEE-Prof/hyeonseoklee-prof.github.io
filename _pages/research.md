## Research Vision
### From Crop Ecophysiology to Predictive and Deployable Technologies

- We investigate how crops perceive and respond to environmental conditions throughout their life cycle, from vegetative growth to heading, grain filling, and harvest.
- We translate physiological and ecological mechanisms into quantitative indicators, predictive models, and crop production technologies.

## Integrated Research Approach

- Field experimentation, physiological analysis, and molecular, transcriptomic, and metabolic profiling
- Digital phenotyping using ground-based sensors, imaging platforms, drones, and satellite data
- Integration of crop models, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence
- Development of climate-smart and resource-efficient crop production technologies
