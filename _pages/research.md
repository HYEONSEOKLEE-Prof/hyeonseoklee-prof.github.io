<div class="cppl-page-intro">
  <div class="cppl-eyebrow">Research at CPPL</div>
  <h1>From Crop Ecophysiology to Predictive and Deployable Technologies</h1>
  <p>We connect crop physiology with phenomics, crop modeling, remote sensing, and deployable production technologies.</p>
</div>

<div class="cppl-two-column">
  <section class="cppl-panel">
    <h2>Research Vision</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Investigate how crops perceive and respond to environmental conditions throughout their life cycle, from vegetative growth to heading, grain filling, and harvest.</li>
      <li>Translate physiological and ecological mechanisms into quantitative indicators, predictive models, and crop production technologies.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="cppl-panel">
    <h2>Integrated Research Approach</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Field experimentation, physiological analysis, and molecular, transcriptomic, and metabolic profiling</li>
      <li>Digital phenotyping using ground-based sensors, imaging platforms, drones, and satellite data</li>
      <li>Integration of crop models, remote sensing, and artificial intelligence</li>
      <li>Development of climate-smart and resource-efficient crop production technologies</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>

<h2 class="cppl-section-title">Core Research Areas</h2>
<div class="cppl-research-grid cppl-research-grid-detail">
  <article class="cppl-research-card">
    <div class="cppl-card-number">01</div>
    <h3>Crop Physiology</h3>
    <p><em>Phenology, photosynthesis, carbon metabolism, and environmental responses</em></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Environmental regulation of crop phenology and yield formation</li>
      <li>Photosynthetic and carbon-metabolic responses to environmental variation</li>
      <li>Physiological and molecular responses across crop developmental stages</li>
    </ul>
  </article>
  <article class="cppl-research-card">
    <div class="cppl-card-number">02</div>
    <h3>Climate-Smart Crop Production</h3>
    <p><em>Climate stress, biostimulants, resource efficiency, and ratoon multi-harvest systems</em></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Heat, low-light, drought, flooding, ozone, and related climate stresses</li>
      <li>Biostimulant-based stress mitigation and treatment optimization</li>
      <li>Resource-efficient production and ratoon-based multi-harvest strategies</li>
    </ul>
  </article>
  <article class="cppl-research-card">
    <div class="cppl-card-number">03</div>
    <h3>Digital Phenomics &amp; Modeling</h3>
    <p><em>Imaging, sensors, artificial intelligence, and crop modeling</em></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Image- and sensor-based indicators of crop growth and development</li>
      <li>Crop-model integration for phenology, growth, and productivity prediction</li>
      <li>Artificial-intelligence-assisted data integration and decision support</li>
    </ul>
  </article>
  <article class="cppl-research-card">
    <div class="cppl-card-number">04</div>
    <h3>Satellite-Based Crop Monitoring &amp; Modeling</h3>
    <p><em>Remote sensing, crop models, growth monitoring, and yield prediction</em></p>
    <ul>
      <li>Satellite-based monitoring of crop growth and field variability</li>
      <li>Integration of remote sensing and crop models</li>
      <li>Regional growth and yield prediction</li>
    </ul>
  </article>
</div>
